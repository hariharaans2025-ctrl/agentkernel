## Review of the returned repairs

- Nano exhausted its output cap and returned no complete accepted repair. Verification was not reached.
- Super moved the barrier before acquiring locks, but left opposing acquisition orders in place. Its claim that the patch established a consistent order was incorrect. The candidate compiled but deadlocked in all 100 verification runs.
- Ultra released the initial mutex before the barrier and then acquired A followed by B in both threads. The candidate compiled and passed all 100 verification runs. This establishes a successful campaign for this fixture, not semantic equivalence or general model superiority.
- Sanitizers were not configured for this fixture; zero reported races does not establish race freedom.
- Exactly one completion request per model, zero retries. Total usage: 6,074 input and 4,529 output tokens (10,603 total); reasoning is included in output usage. Inference costs remain unknown because pricing was not configured.
- Remote regression validation: 221 passed, 8 deselected. Deployment: agentkernel-project:20261003t114108z.
