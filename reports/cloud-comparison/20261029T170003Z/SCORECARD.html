What this supports

This comparison measures this suite and implementation. An adaptive route can recover smaller-model failures while spending more than a direct larger-model call. A small single-pass suite cannot establish statistical superiority or production generalization.

Protocol and interpretation

Super uses a 4,096-token output cap; Ultra uses 8,192 in both standalone and adaptive arms. Each call is limited to 12,000 input tokens. Maximum 32 paid calls, with an established aggregate charge ceiling below $1. No post-result prompt, source, cap or policy adjustments. Two independent jobs could run concurrently, so latency includes shared-service/network effects.
